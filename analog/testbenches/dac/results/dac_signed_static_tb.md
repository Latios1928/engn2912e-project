## Open points

- Weight format: the digital side is sign-magnitude with a 3-bit binary magnitude; `DAC_signed` wraps the 1:2:3:4 `DAC` with a separate sign pin. A matching cell would steer `DAC_binary`'s x1/x2/x4 branches with s = bit 3.
- Dynamic behaviour (charge per input spike when `en` switches, injection, real spike shapes) needs a pulse testbench.
