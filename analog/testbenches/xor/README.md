# XOR on the 2-2-1 `snn_analog`

XOR needs inhibition: with excitatory weights only, the network's output is a monotone function
of its inputs, and XOR (= OR and not AND) is not. The 2-2-1 network computes it with one hidden
neuron as an OR and one as an AND that inhibits the output:

```
A ── in 0 ─┬─► hid 0 (OR,  +7 from each input) ──(+1)──┐
           │                                            ├─► out = A XOR B
B ── in 1 ─┴─► hid 1 (AND, +2 from each input) ──(−7)──┘
```

The weights are the chip's normal sign-magnitude words (bit 3 = sign, bits 2:0 = magnitude,
−7…+7, word map of `mock/snn_analog.v`). XOR is programmed through the register file like any
other weight set: `mixed/` scenarios 6–9 do exactly that, and pass.

This folder is the 2-2-1 port of the 3-5-1 study on branch `xor-inhibition` (commits b8612ed,
ba1eddc). That study's network-level results stay on the branch; its DPI-level sizing results
(`option2_sizing*`, `coincidence_*`, `../dpi/results/dpi_sizing_*`) do not depend on the network
and are copied here.

## Circuit

| Cell | What it is |
|---|---|
| `analog/neuron/DAC_sm.sch/.sym` | Sign-magnitude synapse DAC: three binary branches 1:2:4 on `w[2:0]` (unit 1u/8u source, 0.22u/0.28u switch, as `DAC.sch`), the enable switch `Men`, and the `DAC_signed` steering pair with `w[3]` as the sign: 0 → `Isum_e`, 1 → `Isum_i`. 7 units = 105 nA at most. |
| `analog/neuron/DPI_sized.sch` | `DPI.sch` with all four transistors at **W 1.76 µm, L 2.24 µm** (×8 in W and L; ×4 on the branch, see decision 4). |
| `analog/neuron/DPI_inh.sch` | `DPI_sized` plus a 1:1 nFET mirror that sinks its output from the membrane (unchanged from the branch). |
| `analog/snn/snn_analog.sch/.sym` | Generated by `analog/snn/gen_snn_analog.py`: 8 `DAC_sm`, 5 `DPI_sized`, 3 `DPI_inh` (hidden and output neurons), 5 `neuron_sized`. The inputs have no inhibitory DPI: their inhibitory DAC current goes to VDD, so a negative input weight gives no input (the mock clamps at 0 instead). New bias pins `VT_inh`, `Vthr_in`, `VT_in`, `Vthr_hid`, `VT_hid`; the Verilog ports (`w`, `pulse`, `spike`) are unchanged. |

## Decisions

Early sweeps ran with the testbench defaults of that moment; the values that matter are in the
tables below and in each result file's overrides.

### 1. Separate DPI biases per layer (shared biases fail)

The three layers use the DPI in three modes: the inputs as relays (one spike per input pulse),
the hidden layer in threshold mode (one input gives no output, two give a large one: the AND),
the output as a slow integrator. With one bias pair for all DPIs (`gen_snn_analog.py --biases
shared`), sweeps of gain, τ, inhibitory τ and weights (`grid_sh1`, `grid_sh3`) found no point
where the relays fire once per pulse and the AND stays silent for one input: the relays go from
silent (weight 4) to 2–4 spikes per pulse (weight 5), and a burst from one relay fires the AND like
a coincidence. The points that pass at tt do so by the output's inhibition winning a race; the
best passes 8 / 20 mismatch samples and 1 / 7 corners (`mc_shared_best`, `xor_shared_best`).
**Decision: per-layer pins** (`--biases layer`, the default). The hidden layer's inhibitory DPIs
use the hidden pair too, so −7 into a hidden neuron is as strong as +7 (mixed scenario 5).

### 2. Relays: one spike per pulse, gain not saturated

The relay count is set by the charge per pulse against the neuron's threshold: 1–2× gives one
spike, less gives one every other pulse, more gives two. In threshold mode that charge depends
exponentially on `VT_in`, so the window is narrow. At `Vthr_in` 2.4 V (`grid_relay_lin`): 0.67 V
two spikes per pulse, 0.68–0.71 V one, 0.72 V every other pulse, about 40 mV wide.

At `Vthr_in` 2.0–2.3 V the relay DPI's gain saturates and the count no longer depends on it
(`grid_L4`, `grid_L5`). That works at tt, but the saturated output is set by headroom, not by
bias currents, so it does not follow the bias replicas across corners: with `Vthr_in` 2.2 V the
replica corners failed at ss, ff and fs, all on relays, and keeping the neuron biases or `Vthr_in`
as fixed voltages did not help (`bias_replica_keep_*`). **Decision: `Vthr_in` 2.4 V** (gain
unsaturated, set by its replica), `VT_in` at the window centre, 0.695 V.

### 3. AND: weights +2 instead of +1

With +1 per input the AND's first spike comes 400–800 µs into the (1,1) case, about as late as
the output's first spike, so under mismatch the output often wins the race: 23 / 30 at `VT_hid`
0.62 V (`mc_and1`). Doubling the AND weights and raising the hidden τ current to match
(`VT_hid` 0.655 V) keeps the one-input/two-input ratio, so the logic is unchanged, but doubles
the current above threshold: the AND fires about 50 µs into (1,1) (`grid_andw`, `grid_andw2`).
The race is no longer close, and the output gain can rise: every point in `VT_hid` 0.65–0.66 V ×
`Vthr` 2.68–2.74 V passes the strict check at tt. +3 per input is too much (the AND fires on one
input across the τ range tried). **Decision: +2, `VT_hid` 0.655 V, `Vthr` 2.70 V.**

### 4. DPI sizing: ×8 instead of ×4

Same 30 mismatch samples (seeds 7700–7729), XOR at tt, at the operating point of the time:

| DPI size (all DPIs) | Output correct | Failures | File |
|---|---|---|---|
| ×4 (0.88/1.12 µm, branch) | 22 / 30 | 4 relays, 3 (1,1) races, 1 output too weak | `mc_L6` |
| ×8 (1.76/2.24 µm), same biases | 27 / 30 | 3 relays at 2 spikes per pulse | `mc_L8_k2` |
| ×8, relay τ re-centred | 28 / 30 | 2 relays | `mc_x8` |

The race and weak-output failures go away with the larger DPIs, so at ×4 the DPIs were the main
mismatch source. The transistors are small next to the DPI's 1 pF capacitor, so ×8 costs little
area. W/L is unchanged, so the nominal currents barely move (windows shift by about 10 mV).
`run_xor.py --dpi-scale k` tries other sizes without editing the cell.

### 5. Current-referenced biases with 16-unit replicas

A subthreshold bias cannot be a fixed voltage: with the operating point below as fixed voltages,
XOR passes only at tt (`xor_fixed_final`: ss and 0 °C fire the AND on one input, fs makes the
relays burst, ff, sf and 85 °C silence them). `bias_replica.py` makes every bias except the
comparator threshold `VT` from a diode-connected copy of the transistor it drives (same type, W, L
and rail; the DPI replicas follow `DPI_sized`'s size from the netlist), fed by a current
calibrated at tt and scaled with Iref. Each subthreshold replica is 16 unit copies in parallel at
16× the current: same voltage, 1/4 of the mismatch, since one replica serves a whole layer.
The three strong-inversion neuron biases (`VK`, `VNA`, `VKup`) keep one copy (16× would cost about
1 mW).

The currents are still ideal sources here. On chip they need a bias generator: mirrors from one
master current with ratios from about 0.1× (output DPI) to 1200× (`VK`) Iref. Not designed yet
(see Open).

## Running

```bash
python3 analog/snn/gen_snn_analog.py                  # regenerate the network (after a cell pin change)
python3 analog/testbenches/xor/gen_xor_tb.py          # regenerate xor_tb.sch (after a symbol pin change)
python3 analog/testbenches/xor/run_xor.py xor                     # 4 cases, tt (~20 s)
python3 analog/testbenches/xor/run_xor.py xor --corners all       # 7 corners/temperatures
python3 analog/testbenches/xor/run_xor.py mc --mc 30              # mismatch, ~3 min
python3 analog/testbenches/xor/run_xor.py grid --grid p_vtau_hid=0.65,0.66 --grid c4+c5=2,3
python3 analog/testbenches/xor/bias_replica.py                    # replicas, 7 corners
python3 analog/testbenches/xor/bias_replica.py --mc 30            # replicas, mismatch
python3 analog/testbenches/xor/bias_replica.py --iref-scale 0.85,1.15
make -C mixed run                                                 # register file + network, scenarios 1-9 (~16 min)
```

Options: `--set NAME=VALUE` (any testbench `.param`: weights `c0`–`c7`, biases `p_*`), `--pulses`,
`--dpi-scale`, `--tag` (result file suffix), `--jobs`; `bias_replica.py` also `--replica-n`,
`--strong-n`, `--keep NET,...`, `--corners ss,ff`. Results: `results/<suite>[_tag].md/.csv`.
**Pass** = the output fires for (0,1) and (1,0) and never for (0,0) and (1,1). **Strict** = pass,
every relay fires once per input pulse, and the AND is silent for one input and fires for two.

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `xor_tb.sch` | Full `snn_analog` in plain ngspice: signed weights `c0`–`c7`, pulses from `ina`/`inb`, membranes start at 0 V. Generated by `gen_xor_tb.py` |
| `run_xor.py` | Suites `xor` (corners), `mc` (mismatch, 4 cases per sample), `grid` (parameter product; `c0+c1=...` sets several at once) |
| `bias_replica.py` | Every bias except `VT` made by a diode replica (N unit copies) fed by a current ∝ Iref |
| `hidden_tb.sch`, `inh_cell_tb.sch` | Branch testbenches with the old cells (`DAC`, plain `DPI`); used by the sizing scripts |
| `size_option2.py`, `scan_coincidence.py`, `../dpi/size_dpi.py` | Branch DPI sizing studies (DPI level; their result files are here) |

## Pitfalls

- Maximum step 0.1 µs (at 0.5 µs the neuron produced false spikes at relay edges; `run_xor.py`
  counts single-step membrane jumps as `spurious`).
- Testbench `.param`s are `p_*`: a parameter named like a net makes ngspice rename the net.
- `.ic` starts the membranes and spike outputs at 0 V; without it the operating point of the
  sized network does not converge (also in `mixed/snn_ms_tb.sch`).
- Check strict, not only pass: an output can be right while a hidden neuron is wrong (an AND
  firing on one input, cancelled by luck in a race).
- A relay that fires twice per pulse looks like a coincidence to the AND neuron.

## Open

- **Bias generator**: mirrors from one master current to the replica currents (ratios about
  0.1×–1200× Iref), with the mirrors' own mismatch; an external reference pin or trim for the
  master current. The strong-inversion replicas (`VK`, `VNA`) could use a smaller W/L at a
  proportionally smaller current.
- Relay window (about 40 mV of `VT_in`): ss with replicas fires every third pulse. Options: a
  smaller capacitor in the relay DPIs (shorter tail, no second spike), a larger τ transistor, or
  driving the relay neuron from its DAC directly.
- Layout: `analog/neuron/layout/SNN.gds` follows `snn_analog_inh.sch`; `DPI_sized.gds` and
  `DPI_inh.gds` are still ×4 (`gen_dpi.py -rd w/l`); `DAC_sm` has no layout.
- `Vlk` not re-swept on this network; output DPI currents are about 100 pA, so check leakage at
  high temperature with the real bias generator.
